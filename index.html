Who We Are

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to provide a platform for the study, teaching, and discussion of God's purpose for humanity, with particular emphasis on the Joshua Hamashiak message, understood in our faith as the promised return of Jesus Christ in human form and the central message of God's end-time revelation.

Through Bible teachings, spiritual inspirations, books, audio messages, videos, and publications, we seek to encourage sincere seekers of truth to understand the mystery of God, prepare for the fulfilment of His promises, and pursue the restoration of humanity to God's original purpose.

OUR VISION

Our vision is to see humanity restored to God's original purpose, as symbolized by the Garden of Eden, and to see believers grow into spiritual maturity through the knowledge of God and the understanding of His end-time purpose.

We envision a people who recognize the significance of the Joshua Hamashiak message, prepare themselves for the end-time harvest, and look forward to the fulfilment of God's promises concerning the restoration of His nature in humanity and the establishment of His divine purpose, which is the kingdom of God on earth according to Daniel 2:44.

OUR CORE BELIEFS

01

God's Original Purpose

We believe that God has an original purpose for humanity and that understanding this purpose is essential to our spiritual journey and restoration of the kingdom which Adam lost.

02

The Joshua Hamashiak Message

We believe that Joshua Hamashiak is central to God's end-time revelation and represents, according to our understanding of Scripture, the promised return of Jesus Christ in human form.

03

The Revelation of God's Mystery

We study biblical prophecies concerning the end time, particularly Revelation 10:4–7, which speaks of the seven thunders and the finishing of the mystery of God.

04

The Preparation of the Saints

We emphasize faith and belief, obedience, spiritual growth, and readiness as we seek to understand God's purpose and prepare for the fulfilment of His promises.

05

The End-Time Harvest

We study biblical teachings concerning the harvest, inspired by the Joshua Hamashiak message, the gathering of God's people, and the fulfilment of His end-time purpose.

06

The Restoration of Humanity

We look forward to humanity's restoration to God's original purpose, with the Garden of Eden serving as a biblical symbol of that divine intention.

07

The Study of Scripture and Open Discussion

We encourage sincere questions, careful study of the Scriptures, respectful discussion, and continued growth in the knowledge of God.

OUR INVITATION

We invite you to explore the message, read the Scriptures, listen to teachings, watch videos, study our publications, and participate in meaningful discussions.

Come with a sincere heart, ask questions, and join us as we unravel a deeper understanding of God's purpose for humanity.
